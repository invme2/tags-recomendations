QUICK ANSWERS

Before you buy

Will it actually cool me down?
It moves air across your skin, which speeds evaporation. So yes — measurably cooler within seconds, especially at neck.
How long does the battery really last?
Low: 12h. Mid: 7h. High: 4h. Mixed real-world use: ~6-8h.
Is it loud in meetings?
On low (35dB) it's quieter than a refrigerator and won't trigger mic background noise filters.
Will it tangle long hair?
No — the airflow comes from an enclosed duct, not exposed blades. Hair stays out.
Can I wear it on a bike?
Yes — it stays put on the neck via the rounded hook. Tested up to ~15 mph.
